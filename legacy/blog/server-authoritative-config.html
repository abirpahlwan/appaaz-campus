Enterprise HR on Odoo: roles, attendance and leave

HR App · enterprise systems · ~5 min read

An HR app is not a form with a database behind it. It is roles, approvals and audit trails — and Odoo makes a solid backend if you treat it as one.

At Metamorphosis Ltd (Apr 2025 — present) I build an HR app connected to Odoo as the backend: role-based login and access, attendance management and leave management, plus enterprise-level support and Odoo upgrades.

Roles first, screens second

Who can see whom, who approves what, and what happens when someone changes teams — those answers shape the data model before any UI gets drawn. Getting roles right early keeps every later feature (attendance, leave, reports) from turning into a special case.

Attendance and leave are state machines

Clock-ins, corrections, leave requests, approvals, balances: each one is a small workflow with its own states and transitions. Modelling them explicitly keeps the app predictable for HR staff and auditable for everyone else.

Upgrades and support are part of the product

Enterprise work doesn't end at launch. Odoo upgrades, customer support, and bridging business needs with engineering tasks are the ongoing half — along with coaching teammates on SDLC and engineering best practices so the whole team ships steadily.
